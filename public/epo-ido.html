How it works

Ido and Esperanto are closely related, so most of the translation is word-for-word lookup in the Ido–Esperanto dictionary (Vortaro), with transfer rules for endings, articles and word order. The dictionaries and rules are open source.

Tips for better results

Translate whole sentences rather than isolated words, so the transfer rules can pick the right endings and articles. Words the engine does not know are marked with * in the output; for a single word with its part of speech and related forms, look it up in the dictionary instead.
