Ido → English translator

Translate Ido text into English for free. The translation is done by a neural model, NLLB-200 (600M parameters) fine-tuned for Ido, which runs in your browser or, if you prefer, on our server. This language pair is in beta (experimental): check the result before you rely on it.

How it works

The neural engine is NLLB-200, Meta's multilingual translation model, fine-tuned for Ido on Ido–Esperanto sentence pairs. It reads Ido and writes English with the same model that writes Esperanto, so it translates Ido to English directly, one sentence at a time. There is no rule-based (Apertium) engine for English: Apertium covers only Ido ↔ Esperanto.

In your browser or on our server

The neural engine can run in two ways. You can download the model once (about 610 MB for Ido → English), which your browser keeps for later visits; translation then happens on your device and your text is not sent anywhere. Or you can run the same model on our server with no download: your text is then sent to it for translation and is not stored or logged.

English → Ido

The other direction works too: swap the languages in the translator. English → Ido goes through Esperanto (English → Esperanto → Ido), which gave better results than translating directly, and needs a larger download (about 990 MB) if you run it in your browser.

Tips for better results

Write whole, complete sentences: the model was trained on sentence pairs and works best on everyday sentences. For a single Ido word with its part of speech and related forms, look it up in the Ido–Esperanto dictionary (Vortaro).
